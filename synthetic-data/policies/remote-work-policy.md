# Synthetic Remote Work Policy

This fictional Demo360 policy supports portfolio demonstrations only.

Eligible synthetic workers may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Discuss team coverage, meeting attendance and equipment before agreeing a schedule. Remote work is not an automatic entitlement and changes require a fresh discussion with the manager.

Use approved equipment, protect credentials and keep worker records out of personal sharing tools. Do not upload real employee or client information to this simulator. The application records employment information but does not approve remote work requests.
