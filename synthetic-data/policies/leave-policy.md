# Leave Policy

This document describes leave at the fictional Asterion Digital Technologies. It is not a real employment policy or legal advice.

Regular workers have 18 planned leave days per simulation year. Request leave at least five working days before the proposed absence. Approval depends on team coverage and the manager's review. The simulator does not currently maintain leave balances or implement leave booking.

For unexpected illness, notify the manager as soon as practical. No medical documents should be uploaded to the policy library. Questions about a real entitlement must go to the actual employer's HR team.
