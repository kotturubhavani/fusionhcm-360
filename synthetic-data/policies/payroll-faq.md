# Synthetic Payroll FAQ

This guide explains the portfolio payroll simulation. It is not statutory payroll, tax or financial advice.

Gross pay is the saved earnings total before deductions. Net pay equals gross pay less the saved deduction lines. The payroll screen lists each earning and deduction. Amounts must be read from the worker's completed payroll result; this policy does not define an individual's salary or net pay.

The demonstration can prorate earnings over eligible days in a pay period. A completed payroll run stores the result snapshot and cannot be processed twice. Historical results are not recalculated merely because a worker later changes job, department or compensation. No bank payment or external payroll instruction is generated.
