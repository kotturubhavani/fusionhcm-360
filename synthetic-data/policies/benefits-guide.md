# Synthetic Benefits and FBP Guide

This fictional guide describes Flexible Benefits Planning in the portfolio simulator. It is not benefits, tax or legal advice.

Each eligible assignment has a budget generated from the plan's configured percentage of annual base salary. The default demonstration rate is ten percent. Eligibility and budget values are snapshots, not a promise of real benefits. Read the exact amount from the worker's budget record.

Workers choose component amounts within the configured limits. Allocated is the sum of saved elections; remaining is the budget less allocated. A draft can be saved below budget, but submission requires full allocation. Submitted elections become read-only. Closing a plan finalizes its submitted elections. OPEN budgets have not yet been submitted.
